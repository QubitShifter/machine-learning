## Description

Expected: `valid_pixels + sum(excluded_by_reason) == total_aoi_pixels` on one grid, with every pixel counted once.

Actual: `exclusion_counts` used 10 m `total - valid` (30,671) minus native 20 m non-land SCL (7,480) and labelled the remainder 23,191 as `other_or_unclassified`. That remainder was not a physical class.

## Environment

- Scene `S2B_35TLG_20250119_0_L2A`, acquisition `2025-01-19`, baseline 05.11
- Target grid EPSG:32635 10 m, 258×341 = 87,978 pixels
- Native SCL 129×170 (20 m)
- Native optical 257×340
- Python 3.14.4, numpy 2.5.4, rasterio 1.5.2
- Dataset `plovdiv_2023_2025`

## Reproduction

1. Inspect `data/processed/satellite/plovdiv_2023_2025/scenes/S2B_35TLG_20250119_0_L2A/quality_report.json` before revalidation (legacy residual 23191).
2. Run `classify_pixel_exclusions` on reprojected 10 m arrays.

## Evidence

- Legacy report: valid 57307, scl_rejected 7480, other_or_unclassified 23191
- Native 20 m SCL histogram: `{2:1620, 4:789, 5:13661, 6:13, 11:5847}` (21,930 pixels)
- 10 m disjoint counts: snow/ice 23388, dark 6480, SCL nodata 258, water 52, red/NIR nodata 485, blue/green 8, valid 57307

## Root-cause analysis

SCL is 20 m. Optical/NDVI statistics are 10 m after bilinear/nearest reprojection onto a target grid one row/column larger than the native 10 m window. Mixing those histograms is a reporting defect. Snow/ice (SCL 11) is the dominant real exclusion (~23,388 10 m pixels). SCL class 0 (258 pixels) matches the extra target-grid row. Optical nodata (485+8) follows bilinear expansion and source nodata.

## Resolution

Added `ml/validation/pixel_exclusion.py` and wired it into `evaluate_dataset_quality`. Native SCL histograms are retained as diagnostics only. NDVI rasters were not rewritten. Thresholds were not lowered.

## Mathematical or scientific impact

NDVI values, reflectance calibration, and the 70% valid-NDVI gate are unchanged. The January scene remains rejected at 65.14%. Quality reports and cloud-fraction denominators now use 10 m SCL. This does not create soil-moisture or rainfall changes.

## Validation

`tests/test_pixel_exclusion.py` passed, including January 2025 conservation `57307+23388+6480+485+258+52+8=87978`. Dataset `validate` rewrote quality reports only (NDVI GeoTIFFs unchanged). `pixel_conservation_ok` true and `stored_valid_mask_agrees` true. Independently verified at `2026-10-22T07:45:24Z`.
