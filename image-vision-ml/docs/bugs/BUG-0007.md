## Description

Expected: reports distinguish radiometric calibration from overall scene usability.

Actual: `S2B_35TLG_20250119_0_L2A` had overall `rejected` and calibration `verified` / audit `current_status` `valid`. That pair is scientifically allowed but the field names did not say so.

## Environment

API `SceneQualityResponse` and `calibration_audit.json`. Frontend satellite explorer previously showed a single Status.

## Reproduction

`test_rejected_scene_can_keep_verified_calibration`.

## Evidence

January quality_report `quality_status=rejected`, `calibration.validation_status=verified`.

## Root-cause analysis

Documentation/API naming, not a wrong coefficient. Missing STAC hrefs are reported when the audit record has no `assets`; they are not invented (`BUG-0009`).

## Resolution

Explicit fields on scene quality and calibration audit. Explorer shows Scene quality and Calibration separately.

## Mathematical or scientific impact

None of the NDVI or reflectance equations changed.

## Validation

`tests/test_calibration_audit.py::test_rejected_scene_can_keep_verified_calibration` passed. After `validate`, January scene report has `scene_quality_status=rejected` and `calibration_status=verified`. Independently verified at `2026-10-22T07:45:24Z`.
