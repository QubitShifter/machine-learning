## Description

Expected: every ingested scene has verified BOA coefficients from STAC flags and raster metadata.

Actual: two scenes remain `requires_review` because stored calibration is conflicting. Plausible NDVI is not used to auto-validate them.

## Environment

- Collection Earth Search `sentinel-2-l2a`
- Dataset `plovdiv_2023_2025`
- Policy `quality.calibration_policy: verified_only`

## Reproduction

`audit_scene_calibration` on a conflicting sidecar with even extreme NDVI stays `requires_review`.

## Evidence

- Dataset calibration audit `unresolved_scene_ids` contains the two scene IDs
- `docs/sentinel2_calibration_validation.md` describes the BOA flag versus advertised offset contradiction

## Root-cause analysis

Source-metadata conflict, not a local arithmetic bug in `to_reflectance`. Sample NDVI cannot resolve which encoding is authoritative for those items.

## Resolution

Deferred. Do not rewrite coefficients. Do not mark the scenes valid. Preserve both as `requires_review`.

## Mathematical or scientific impact

Those two scenes are excluded from validated NDVI series used for modelling eligibility. Other verified scenes are unaffected. No soil-moisture impact (no soil product).

## Validation
