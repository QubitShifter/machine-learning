## CHG-0009 — First agricultural soil-moisture forecasting baselines

**Timestamp:** `2026-10-22T18:38:09Z`
**Phase:** Phase 4.1
**Type:** feature
**Status:** Verified

## Reason

Phase 4.0 produced a leakage-safe research table. The next scientific question is whether learned models beat persistence on chronological holdout years for modeled 0–7 cm ERA5-Land soil moisture.

## Previous behavior

No trained soil-moisture forecast models. Metrics and persistence were specified as contracts on fixtures. `suitable_for_modeling` was false. Operational agricultural forecasting remained planned.

## New behavior

Experiment `plovdiv_soil_forecast_baselines_v1` (pipeline 4.1.0) on dataset `plovdiv_agricultural_ml_2023_2025` v4.0.0:

- Target `swvl1` (0–7 cm), horizons 1/3/7, issue cutoff \(D-1\), label `research_reanalysis`.
- Feature sets A/B/C; D satellite disabled because acquisition timestamps are synthesized midnight UTC.
- Complete-case rows; missing rainfall is never filled with 0. Preprocessors fitted on 2023 only.
- Persistence, training-year monthly seasonal reference, Ridge (alpha grid on 2024 MAE), HistGradientBoostingRegressor (small 2024 grid, seed 42).
- Freeze at `2026-10-22T18:38:09Z`, fingerprint `bc94975fc52834290c85c3cd9f666b1569567ad2a625ece6ea7e7601deb926f8`.
- 2025 scored once at `2026-10-22T18:38:17Z`; a repeat preserved the original record.
- Read-only GET `/api/v1/ml/experiments`. No `/predict`. No irrigation advice.
- Frontend research charts deferred.

Validation 2024 matched C: Ridge skill +0.056 / +0.055 / +0.101 at h=1/3/7. Test 2025: persistence wins h=1 and h=3; Ridge skill +0.029 at h=7 only. Seasonal is worse than persistence.
