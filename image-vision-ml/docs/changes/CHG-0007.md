## CHG-0007 — Soil Analysis contextual workspaces

**Timestamp:** `2026-10-22T16:35:43Z`
**Phase:** Soil Analysis UX Refinement
**Type:** enhancement
**Status:** Verified

## Reason

Year/month selectors sat at the top of Soil Analysis, the year-over-year chart occupied the middle, and detailed depth and time-series charts sat farther down. Changing the detailed year required scrolling away from the charts it affected.

## Previous behavior

A single “Soil time selection” card at the top owned URL `year`/`month` and daily/monthly aggregation. Historical Soil Moisture — Year-over-Year Comparison followed. Depth profile, soil-water time series, rainfall versus soil moisture, antecedent precipitation, and a large ERA5-Land grid card came last.

## New behavior

Two workspaces:

1. **Historical Comparison** first: year-over-year chart, comparison depth, year visibility toggles, January–December lines, interannual Δθ, seasonal means, compact provenance. Comparison state stays inside `YearOverYearSoilChart`.
2. **Detailed Soil Analysis** second: year, month/full year, daily/monthly aggregation, and layer selectors immediately above the depth profile, time series, rainfall chart, and compact metadata.

**Explore this year** (explicit click, not hover) sets the detailed year, optionally a month from a chart point, and smooth-scrolls to `#detailed-soil-analysis`. Comparison toggles are preserved. Monthly API data is reused; selection state is not shared.
