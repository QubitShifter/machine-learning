## CHG-0006 — Year-over-year soil-moisture chart

**Timestamp:** `2026-10-22T16:14:22Z`
**Phase:** Soil Analysis UI Enhancement
**Type:** feature
**Status:** Verified

## Reason

Users needed to compare the same calendar months across 2023–2025 on the Soil Analysis page after the ERA5-Land soil archive became complete.

## Previous behavior

Soil Analysis showed a single selected year as a daily or monthly time series plus a depth-profile bar chart. Years 2023–2025 were hardcoded in the year dropdown. There was no overlay of multiple years on a January–December axis.

## New behavior

A year-over-year chart uses `GET /api/v1/soil/datasets/{id}/monthly`. X-axis is January–December. Lines are derived years. Default depth is the weighted 0–100 cm profile. Years can be toggled. Missing months are gaps. Tooltips include complete-day counts and modeled-reanalysis provenance. Δθ = θ_yearB − θ_yearA is shown for a selected month. Seasonal DJF uses December of Y with January–February of Y+1. Existing daily/monthly charts remain.

## Scientific impact

None on stored values. Chart reads processed monthly means. January 2023 θ_{0-100} = 0.3029981553682717; January 2025 = 0.3734779265619093; Δθ = 0.0704797711936376 m³/m³. Raw NetCDF, manifests, and processed CSVs were not rewritten.

## Backward-compatibility impact

Additive UI. Monthly API unchanged. Existing soil charts and layer selector remain.
