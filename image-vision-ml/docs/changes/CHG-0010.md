## CHG-0010 — ML Training Laboratory

**Timestamp:** `2026-10-22T19:18:10Z`
**Phase:** Phase 4.1-UI
**Type:** feature
**Status:** Verified

## Reason

Phase 4.1 produced frozen forecasting artifacts. Researchers needed a page that combines those results with honest training-process status and clearly labeled educational mathematics, without implying live training or operational skill.

## Previous behavior

No `/ml/training` route. Data Quality showed the research-dataset summary only. Experiment GET APIs listed metrics and predictions but not dataset counts, feature catalog, training telemetry state, or provenance. Predictions navigation stayed disabled.

## New behavior

Sidebar **ML Training Lab** opens `/ml/training`. Selectors for experiment, horizon, algorithm, feature set, and partition persist across Overview, Dataset, Algorithms, Training Process, Evaluation, Mathematics, and Experiment Provenance. Counts and metrics come from stored artifacts. Missing `training_events.jsonl` is shown as unavailable. Educational demos are labeled. GET `/dataset`, `/features`, `/training`, and `/provenance` are read-only. `suitable_for_modeling`, `is_ground_truth`, and operational flags remain false.

## Scientific impact

No new fitted models. No rewrite of experiment artifacts or raw archives. Displayed MAE/skill are the Phase 4.1 files. Educational OLS/GD/Ridge/boosting sliders use synthetic data only.

## Backward-compatibility impact

Additive read-only API routes. Existing `/experiments`, `/metrics`, `/predictions`, and research-dataset endpoints remain. Predictions nav still disabled. AppShell Suspense moved around `Outlet` so the sidebar stays visible while lazy pages load.
